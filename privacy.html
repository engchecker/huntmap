Privacy policy

Hunt Access NZ · Last updated 23 November 2026

Hunt Access NZ is a map for New Zealand hunters. It's built to keep your information on your own phone. There are no user accounts, no advertising and no analytics or tracking.

What stays on your device

  • Your waypoints, tracks, routes, hunt log entries and photos, landowner permissions, trip plans and saved offline areas.
  • Your settings and the map key used to load LINZ basemaps.

This information is stored only in the app's storage on your device. We don't receive or keep a copy. Deleting the app (or clearing the site's data in your browser) removes it.

Your location

The app uses your location, when you allow it, to show where you are, give land status and boundary alerts, record tracks and guide you back to a point. Location is processed on your device and isn't sent to us.

In the Android app, track recording can carry on when the screen is off. While it does, Android shows a notification, and recording stops when you tap "Stop & save".

Information sent to map and data services

To show maps, land status and weather, the app asks the following public services for data about the area you're looking at. These requests include map coordinates and the usual technical details any web request carries, such as your IP address. They don't include your name or any account details.

  • Toitū Te Whenua Land Information New Zealand (LINZ): basemaps, imagery, elevation and land records.
  • Department of Conservation: conservation land, hunting areas, huts, tracks and pesticide operations (through ArcGIS Online, operated by Esri).
  • Herenga ā Nuku Aotearoa, the Outdoor Access Commission: public access areas (through ArcGIS Online).
  • Open-Meteo: weather and wind forecasts for the point you select.

Each service handles requests under its own privacy policy.

Things you choose to share

When you share a trip plan, GPX file, trip report or trip-intentions message, the app hands it to the app you pick (such as Messages, email or Google Maps). What happens next is up to you and that app. Opening directions sends the destination to Apple Maps or Google Maps.

Children

The app isn't directed at children and doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone.

Your rights

We don't hold personal information about you, so there's nothing for us to correct or delete. If you have a question or concern under the Privacy Act 2020, contact us below.

Changes
